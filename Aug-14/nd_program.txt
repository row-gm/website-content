<h2 class="row-h2">How this group fits the plan</h2>

<p class="row-body" style="margin:0 0 16px;">ROW groups share one development plan. It sets out what we are developing, how training is built, and why a swimmer&rsquo;s progress does not run in a straight line.</p>

<p class="row-body" style="margin:0;">How the programs connect as swimmers develop is on <a class="row-link" href="https://www.rowswimming.ca/page/programs" target="_blank">Programs Overview</a>. What we are developing, and why, is in <a class="row-link" href="https://www.rowswimming.ca/page/our-development-plan" target="_blank">Our Development Plan</a>.</p>

<div class="row-card">
<h2 class="row-h2">What to Expect</h2>

<ul style="margin:0;padding:0 0 0 18px;">
	<li class="row-body" style="margin:0 0 12px;"><strong>Expert in your own performance.</strong> ROW&rsquo;s goal is to develop swimmers who are experts in their own performance. National Development is where that standard is fully expected.</li>
	<li class="row-body" style="margin:0 0 12px;"><strong>All four development dimensions at championship level.</strong> Physical, Technical, Mental, and Character are all explicit coaching targets. Technical precision &mdash; underwaters, turn speed, race-pace mechanics &mdash; is one of them.</li>
	<li class="row-body" style="margin:0 0 12px;"><strong>Training organized around championship peaking.</strong> The season is built in phases, with Swim Ontario and Swimming Canada events as the targets. Taper timing, meet selection, and race prep are all structured around those dates.</li>
	<li class="row-body" style="margin:0 0 12px;"><strong>Versatility and event profile.</strong> All four strokes are tracked. The Goal Setting Tool and regular coach conversations keep you clear on where your times are strong and where the gaps are.</li>
	<li class="row-body" style="margin:0 0 12px;"><strong>Goal setting across the season.</strong> Targets are set with the tool and refined with your coach. Reviewing and adjusting across the season is part of how self-knowledge develops.</li>
	<li class="row-body" style="margin:0 0 12px;"><strong>Mental skills and race strategy.</strong> Executing a race plan under pressure, managing a taper, and competing at your best on the day are all trained &mdash; not assumed.</li>
	<li class="row-body" style="margin:0;"><strong>GoSwim and underwater video.</strong> Coaches use GoSwim reference clips and the underwater camera in sessions for individual stroke feedback at the margins.</li>
</ul>
