<h2 class="row-h2">About this group</h2>

<p class="row-body" style="margin:0;">For swimmers up to 18 who want to keep swimming without the competitive commitment. Technique, fitness, and a group to belong to. Two options, and the only difference is when they train. <strong>REC PM</strong> is three evenings a week at Cameron Heights. <strong>REC AM</strong> is three mornings a week at the Rec Complex.</p>
