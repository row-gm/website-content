ROW Swim Club

2026–27 Registration Fees

A full breakdown of what's included in your fees, how payments are structured, and the schedule for the 2026–27 season.

The ROW Board of Directors took the opportunity this season to do a full reset on fees across all programs. While fighting increasing costs on all fronts, the Board reviewed program and coaching costs and trends over the past several seasons. Adjustments have been made to better reflect what it costs to deliver each group, and across all groups, fees have decreased by 2.3%.

Fee adjustments were not automatically indexed, nor did we pass on increases from our facility and governing bodies.

Understanding Your Fees

Your season fees have three parts. Here is what each one covers.

Registration Fee
An annual fee paid once at the start of each season. It covers your swimmer's club membership and registration with Swim Ontario and Swimming Canada. This fee is $425 for all competitive and TOPS groups, and $200 for REC groups. ROW Swim Academy has no registration fee.
Training Fees
Covers the cost of coaching, pool time, and program delivery for your group throughout the full season. The amount varies by group and reflects the level and cost of coaching involved.
Prepaid Meet Fees
For competitive groups, meet entry fees are collected at the start of the season based on a typical competition schedule. This covers your swimmer's entries for the year without requiring separate payments for each meet. Prepaid meet fees have been increased this season to accurately reflect a typical year for each group. REC and ROW Swim Academy groups have no prepaid meet fees.
Total Due
The full season amount owing — the sum of Registration Fee, Training Fees, and Prepaid Meet Fees. This is divided across an initial payment in September and equal monthly payments thereafter.

Payment Schedule

Fees are collected in two stages: an initial payment in September 2026, followed by equal monthly payments starting October 15.

Sept 2026
First payment — equal to your group's Registration Fee. Competitive and TOPS groups pay $425; REC groups pay $200. ROW Swim Academy families pay the full amount at this step.
Oct 15 onwards
Monthly payments — the remaining balance is split into equal instalments. Competitive groups (National Development through Junior Development 2) have 10 payments. TOPS and REC groups have 9 payments. See the table below for the monthly amount for your group.
Monthly payments are a billing schedule, not equal monthly programming. All groups begin training in September and continue through to the end of June, with groups on provincial or national competition schedules running into July. The split between a September registration payment and monthly instalments is simply how fees are collected — it does not mean programming is equal in all months.

Payment Methods

Choose the payment method that works best for your family.

1
Full in advance
Pay the full season balance at registration. No further payments required.
2
Monthly by Automatic Funds Transfer
Your bank account is debited automatically on the 15th of each month.
3
Monthly by Member Initiated e-Transfer
You send an e-Transfer on or before the 15th of each month.
4
Monthly Automatic Credit Card payment*
Your credit card is charged automatically on the 15th of each month.
* A processing fee applies to automatic credit card payments.

One payment method must be committed to at the start of the season. To change your method during the season, contact the ROW Club Office at office@rowswimming.ca.

What Gets Billed When

Not everything is collected at the start of the season. Here is how billing works across the year.

Registration & Training Fees
Billed at the start of the season. ROW Swim Academy fees are billed at the start of each session.
Swim Meet Fees
Billed at the time each meet happens, based on your swimmer's actual entries.
Special Events
Pre-payment is required for club events such as the year-end banquet, bowling nights, and similar activities.
Team Travel Meets
Costs for team travel competitions are handled separately and are not included in your prepaid meet fees.
You can view your ROW account balance at any time by logging in to your online account on the website.
