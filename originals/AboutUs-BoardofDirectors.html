ROW Swim Club

Board of Directors

ROW is governed by a Board of Directors made up of parent volunteers, elected to three-year terms with a maximum of two consecutive terms. Board meetings are open to all ROW members in good standing.

Club Governance

1 Board members are elected to three-year terms, with a maximum of two consecutive terms.
2 Elections happen at the Annual General Meeting (AGM), typically held in January. Any openings are advertised in advance.
3 Board meetings are open to all ROW members in good standing, usually held the 1st Wednesday of the month.
4 Want an item on the agenda? Submit it to the president at least 2 days before the meeting.

Have a Concern?

Incidents around the pool happen sometimes. Here's the path we ask you to follow:

1 Start with your swimmer's coach, to discuss and resolve the issue directly.
2 Not satisfied with the resolution? Bring your concerns to the Head Coach.
3 Still unresolved? Contact the Member Liaison at memberliaison@rowswimming.ca.
