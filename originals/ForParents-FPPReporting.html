ROW Swim Club

FPP Reporting & Tracking

Here's how FPP activities get recorded and how you'll hear back about your balance.

A great swim club isn’t built from the sidelines; it’s built together.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
1

Your role: report your activities

You are responsible for submitting your completed FPP activities. Submit as soon as possible after the activity — do not wait until the end of the season, as late submissions may not be accepted.

Meet roles & TRAINING

1 ROW-hosted meets (Fall First Try, ROW Novice, Dean Boles, Cunningham Classic) are tracked automatically — nothing to submit. Likewise, all Swimming Canada officials training is automatically tracked and assigned to you.
2 Volunteered at a non-ROW meet? Email officials@rowswimming.ca directly — do not use the self-reporting form for meet roles.

Everything else

1 Committee work, chaperoning, merchandise help, Off the Blocks Meeting, the AGM, club social events, and similar non-swim meet related activities — submit using the self-reporting form below.
2 Include your family name and the email address you use to access the ROW website so points are credited correctly.

Meet questions: officials@rowswimming.ca
Everything else: gm@rowswimming.ca
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
2

Our role: track and report back

ROW tracks FPP points centrally. You don't need to keep your own tally — we'll send balance updates to all members at least three times during the season.

DECEMBER

Mid-season check-in so you know where you stand heading into the new year.

MARCH

Third-quarter update with enough time left to earn any remaining points.

JUNE

Final balance before reporting closes in mid-July.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
3

If you fall short

Families who don't meet their FPP requirement by season's end are charged a financial penalty for every point short, applied to the account after points reporting closes.

The current penalty is $50 per point short of the requirement.

Note: This policy and penalty amount is reviewed by the Board of Directors each season.

Example: a family with a 12-point requirement finishes the season with 8 points earned — 4 points short.
4 × $50 = $200 charged to the account.
