ROW Swim Club

How to Get Involved

ROW is built by the people who show up — coaches, families, officials, and volunteers working together to create a swim club experience worth remembering.

A great swim club isn’t built from the sidelines; it’s built together.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

There are many ways to contribute to ROW — from working as a lane timer at one of our meets to leading a social activity for your swimmer’s group, all the way to connecting us with merchandise or sponsored services that the club needs to operate. Every contribution, big or small, helps us build the kind of club swimmers, families, and coaches are proud to be part of. Most of these opportunities also earn Family Partnership Program (FPP) points.
