<h2 style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:0 0 8px;font-size:24px;">How to use this</h2>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14.5px;color:#152225;line-height:1.65;margin:0 0 10px;">These six names describe what training is doing right now. They are not a calendar and they are not in a fixed order. A phase can repeat as often as the plan needs it. Use the same name for the same purpose in every group, so a swimmer moving from 12&amp;U to senior hears one consistent story.</p>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14.5px;color:#152225;line-height:1.65;margin:0;">Each phase does one of three jobs. <strong>Load</strong> raises training stress. <strong>Unload</strong> lowers it, for three different reasons. <strong>Entry</strong> brings a swimmer back into training.</p>

<div style="background:#FFFFFF;border:1px solid #DAD3C2;border-radius:12px;padding:24px 26px;">
<h2 style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:0 0 8px;font-size:24px;">The six phases</h2>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#4B5B60;line-height:1.65;margin:0 0 14px;">Priority training is what a visitor would see on deck during that phase.</p>

<div style="border:1px solid #DAD3C2;border-radius:10px;overflow:hidden;background:#FFFFFF;box-shadow:0 6px 20px rgba(10,46,63,0.10);">
<div style="display:flex;gap:22px;padding:18px 22px;background:#FFFFFF;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;">
<div style="flex:none;width:190px;">
<div style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.01em;color:#0A2E3F;font-size:15px;line-height:1.2;margin:0 0 5px;">Introduction</div>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13px;font-style:italic;color:#136B77;margin:0 0 8px;">Get ready to train.</div>
<span style="display:inline-block;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:0.08em;background:#0A2E3F;color:#FFFFFF;border-radius:4px;padding:3px 8px;">ENTRY</span>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:12.5px;font-weight:700;color:#4B5B60;margin:9px 0 0;">2 to 4 weeks</div>
</div>

<div style="flex:1;">
<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#152225;line-height:1.6;margin:0 0 8px;">Technique reset, skill drills, and basic dryland movement. Aerobic work returns gradually. Rebuild attendance, sleep, and warm-up routines. Use this phase any time a swimmer returns from four or more weeks away, not just at season start.</div>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:11.5px;letter-spacing:0.03em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#4B5B60;">Volume: low, rising &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Intensity: low</div>
</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:22px;padding:18px 22px;background:#FAF8F2;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;">
<div style="flex:none;width:190px;">
<div style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.01em;color:#0A2E3F;font-size:15px;line-height:1.2;margin:0 0 5px;">Accumulation</div>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13px;font-style:italic;color:#136B77;margin:0 0 8px;">Build the engine.</div>
<span style="display:inline-block;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:0.08em;background:#D64545;color:#FFFFFF;border-radius:4px;padding:3px 8px;">LOAD</span>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:12.5px;font-weight:700;color:#4B5B60;margin:9px 0 0;">4 to 12 weeks</div>
</div>

<div style="flex:1;">
<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#152225;line-height:1.6;margin:0 0 8px;">Aerobic capacity and durability. Long aerobic and threshold sets, high metres, general strength. Hold stroke technique under fatigue. Expect slower race times here. That is planned, and it is the message families need most.</div>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:11.5px;letter-spacing:0.03em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#4B5B60;">Volume: high &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Intensity: low to moderate</div>
</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:22px;padding:18px 22px;background:#FFFFFF;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;">
<div style="flex:none;width:190px;">
<div style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.01em;color:#0A2E3F;font-size:15px;line-height:1.2;margin:0 0 5px;">Consolidation</div>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13px;font-style:italic;color:#136B77;margin:0 0 8px;">Absorb the work.</div>
<span style="display:inline-block;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:0.08em;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;border-radius:4px;padding:3px 8px;">UNLOAD</span>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:12.5px;font-weight:700;color:#4B5B60;margin:9px 0 0;">4 to 7 days</div>
</div>

<div style="flex:1;">
<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#152225;line-height:1.6;margin:0 0 8px;">A short unload so the last block can settle before the next one starts. Metres drop clearly. Quality is held, so sharpness is not lost. Best home for a low-priority meet. This is not a taper and it is not a break. Use it after every hard block.</div>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:11.5px;letter-spacing:0.03em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#4B5B60;">Volume: down 30 to 40% &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Intensity: held</div>
</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:22px;padding:18px 22px;background:#FAF8F2;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;">
<div style="flex:none;width:190px;">
<div style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.01em;color:#0A2E3F;font-size:15px;line-height:1.2;margin:0 0 5px;">Intensification</div>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13px;font-style:italic;color:#136B77;margin:0 0 8px;">Sharpen at race pace.</div>
<span style="display:inline-block;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:0.08em;background:#D64545;color:#FFFFFF;border-radius:4px;padding:3px 8px;">LOAD</span>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:12.5px;font-weight:700;color:#4B5B60;margin:9px 0 0;">3 to 5 weeks</div>
</div>

<div style="flex:1;">
<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#152225;line-height:1.6;margin:0 0 8px;">Race-specific quality. This is where general fitness becomes race fitness. Race pace sets, speed, starts, turns and finishes at full speed, pacing and race strategy. Less volume, harder work, more recovery needed between sessions.</div>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:11.5px;letter-spacing:0.03em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#4B5B60;">Volume: down &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Intensity: high</div>
</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:22px;padding:18px 22px;background:#FFFFFF;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;">
<div style="flex:none;width:190px;">
<div style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.01em;color:#0A2E3F;font-size:15px;line-height:1.2;margin:0 0 5px;">Peaking</div>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13px;font-style:italic;color:#136B77;margin:0 0 8px;">Rest and race fast.</div>
<span style="display:inline-block;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:0.08em;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;border-radius:4px;padding:3px 8px;">UNLOAD</span>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:12.5px;font-weight:700;color:#4B5B60;margin:9px 0 0;">1 to 3 weeks</div>
</div>

<div style="flex:1;">
<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#152225;line-height:1.6;margin:0 0 8px;">The taper. Total work drops clearly while speed stays sharp. Race rehearsal, warm-up routine, and mental prep. Only use this name for a meet named as a peak in advance. Most groups get two or three peaks in a season, not more.</div>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:11.5px;letter-spacing:0.03em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#4B5B60;">Volume: low &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Intensity: sharp, low total work</div>
</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:22px;padding:18px 22px;background:#FAF8F2;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;">
<div style="flex:none;width:190px;">
<div style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.01em;color:#0A2E3F;font-size:15px;line-height:1.2;margin:0 0 5px;">Transition</div>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13px;font-style:italic;color:#136B77;margin:0 0 8px;">Recover and reset.</div>
<span style="display:inline-block;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:0.08em;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;border-radius:4px;padding:3px 8px;">UNLOAD</span>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:12.5px;font-weight:700;color:#4B5B60;margin:9px 0 0;">1 to 2 weeks</div>
</div>

<div style="flex:1;">
<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#152225;line-height:1.6;margin:0 0 8px;">Repair and review before the next cycle. Active recovery, other sports, injury care, season debrief, and goal setting. This is where swimmer reviews against The ROW Way belong. Schedule it in the plan first. If it is left as whatever time remains, it disappears.</div>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:11.5px;letter-spacing:0.03em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#4B5B60;">Volume: low &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Intensity: low</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:0 0 8px;font-size:24px;">Five rules</h2>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#4B5B60;line-height:1.65;margin:0 0 14px;">Everything else is your call as lead coach.</p>

<div style="border:1px solid #DAD3C2;border-radius:10px;overflow:hidden;background:#FFFFFF;box-shadow:0 6px 20px rgba(10,46,63,0.10);">
<div style="display:flex;gap:14px;align-items:flex-start;padding:12px 20px;background:#FFFFFF;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;"><span style="flex:none;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:13px;width:26px;height:26px;border-radius:50%;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;">1</span>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;padding-top:3px;">A load phase is always followed by an unload phase. Never run two load phases back to back.</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:14px;align-items:flex-start;padding:12px 20px;background:#FAF8F2;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;"><span style="flex:none;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:13px;width:26px;height:26px;border-radius:50%;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;">2</span>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;padding-top:3px;">Consolidation follows a load phase and leads back into one. It never follows Peaking.</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:14px;align-items:flex-start;padding:12px 20px;background:#FFFFFF;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;"><span style="flex:none;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:13px;width:26px;height:26px;border-radius:50%;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;">3</span>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;padding-top:3px;">Peaking is only for a designated peak meet, and the stream names it. Regional peaks at Westerns, Provincial at OAG or OSC, National at Trials or Nationals.</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:14px;align-items:flex-start;padding:12px 20px;background:#FAF8F2;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;"><span style="flex:none;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:13px;width:26px;height:26px;border-radius:50%;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;">4</span>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;padding-top:3px;">Transition follows a peak meet or closes a cycle. It is the only phase that resets everything.</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:14px;align-items:flex-start;padding:12px 20px;background:#FFFFFF;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;"><span style="flex:none;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:13px;width:26px;height:26px;border-radius:50%;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;">5</span>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;padding-top:3px;">Introduction follows any break of four weeks or more, for a group or for one swimmer.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div style="border-left:4px solid #3FBFB0;background:#EFFAF8;border-radius:0 10px 10px 0;padding:16px 20px;margin:20px 0 0;font-size:14.5px;color:#152225;font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;line-height:1.6;"><strong>Meets do not change the phase.</strong> Most meets on our calendar sit inside Accumulation or Consolidation. A swimmer can race well without a taper. Tell families this in September, not in November.</div>
</div>

<div style="background:#FFFFFF;border:1px solid #DAD3C2;border-radius:12px;padding:24px 26px;">
<h2 style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:0 0 8px;font-size:24px;">Same words, different mix</h2>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#4B5B60;line-height:1.65;margin:0 0 14px;">The vocabulary does not change by group or by stream. What changes is the share of the season each phase takes, and that follows age.</p>
