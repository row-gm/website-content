<h2 style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:0 0 8px;font-size:24px;">The one rule</h2>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:15px;color:#152225;line-height:1.65;margin:0 0 12px;"><strong>The zone is fixed. The prescription moves.</strong> Everything on this page changes how hard a set is for one swimmer without changing what the set trains. If a change moves a swimmer out of the zone on the board, it has gone too far.</p>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14.5px;color:#152225;line-height:1.65;margin:0;">This is not extra work at the whiteboard. It is one set, written once, with a small number of adjustments made in the lane. Most of them take a coach a few seconds.</p>

<div style="border-left:4px solid #3FBFB0;background:#EFFAF8;border-radius:0 10px 10px 0;padding:16px 20px;margin:20px 0 0;font-size:14.5px;color:#152225;font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;line-height:1.6;"><strong>Change one thing at a time.</strong> Shorten the rest, or add reps, or raise the target. Change two and you no longer know which one worked, and the set often lands in a different zone than the one you wrote.</div>

<h2 style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:0 0 8px;font-size:24px;">Where targets come from</h2>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:15px;color:#152225;line-height:1.65;margin:0 0 12px;">Individual targets should come from a number, not from a feeling about a swimmer. Club-wide tests give you that number. Because every group runs the same tests at the same points in the season, a swimmer&#39;s targets follow them as they move up.</p>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14.5px;color:#152225;line-height:1.65;margin:0;">Which tests we use, when they run, and how a result converts into a Red target or a send-off are covered on the club testing page. Until a swimmer has a current test result, set the lane by ability and adjust by what you see.</p>

<div style="border-left:4px solid #3FBFB0;background:#EFFAF8;border-radius:0 10px 10px 0;padding:16px 20px;margin:20px 0 0;font-size:14.5px;color:#152225;font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;line-height:1.6;"><strong>12&amp;U swimmers do not need pace targets.</strong> Test them, record it, and show them the improvement. Then let them race the clock rather than chase a number in training.</div>
</div>

<div style="background:#FFFFFF;border:1px solid #DAD3C2;border-radius:12px;padding:24px 26px;">
<h2 style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:0 0 8px;font-size:24px;">Running the lane</h2>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#4B5B60;line-height:1.65;margin:0 0 14px;">The reason coaches avoid individual prescriptions is that a lane gets messy. These six habits handle almost all of it.</p>

<div style="border:1px solid #DAD3C2;border-radius:10px;overflow:hidden;background:#FFFFFF;box-shadow:0 6px 20px rgba(10,46,63,0.10);">
<div style="display:flex;gap:16px;padding:15px 20px;background:#FFFFFF;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;"><span style="flex:none;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:13px;width:28px;height:28px;border-radius:50%;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;">1</span>

<div>
<div style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;font-size:13px;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:2px 0 5px;">Order the lane by speed</div>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13.8px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;">Fastest leaves first, five seconds between swimmers. Similar swimmers together means similar cycles, and the lane holds itself together.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:16px;padding:15px 20px;background:#FAF8F2;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;"><span style="flex:none;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:13px;width:28px;height:28px;border-radius:50%;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;">2</span>

<div>
<div style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;font-size:13px;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:2px 0 5px;">Three send-offs maximum</div>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13.8px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;">One per lane, or three across the group. More than that and you are managing a spreadsheet instead of coaching.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:16px;padding:15px 20px;background:#FFFFFF;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;"><span style="flex:none;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:13px;width:28px;height:28px;border-radius:50%;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;">3</span>

<div>
<div style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;font-size:13px;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:2px 0 5px;">Regroup at round breaks</div>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13.8px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;">Rest intervals inside a round, everyone back on the top of the clock between rounds. Drift never grows past one round.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:16px;padding:15px 20px;background:#FAF8F2;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;"><span style="flex:none;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:13px;width:28px;height:28px;border-radius:50%;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;">4</span>
