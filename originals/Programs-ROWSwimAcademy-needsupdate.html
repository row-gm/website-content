Skill Requirements

Beginner

Ages 4–5

Comfortable in the water and able to self-submerge. Able to swim 3–5m on their front, back, or under the water.

Intermediate

Ages 5–8

Comfortable in the water. Able to swim 10m of front crawl and back crawl, and jump into shallow water independently.

Advanced

Ages 5–8

Comfortable in deep water. Able to swim 15–20m of front crawl and back crawl, and jump into deep water.
