ROW Swim Club

Summer Swim Programs

Two ways for swimmers to keep developing over the summer — a short, intensive Performance Camp and an ongoing Summer Swim Team.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

We’re excited to offer summer swim opportunities designed to help athletes continue developing their skills, confidence, and fitness in a fun and focused environment. Our Performance Camp and Summer Swim Team provide excellent training for swimmers who want to improve their technique, endurance, and overall performance throughout the summer.

1

Summer Performance Camp

Dates
July 27–30 (Mon–Thu)
Time
9:00 AM – 4:45 PM
Location
Laurier University Pool
75 University Ave W

Our ROW Summer Performance Camp is designed for competitive swimmers ages 8–15 who are registered with Swim Canada and are proficient in all four competitive strokes. Camp sessions focus on stroke technique, starts, turns, finishes, and race skills, along with structured dryland training.

We are also excited to welcome guest speakers, including a Canadian National Team member, who will lead a swim session and provide mentorship to our athletes.

Camp Program

  • Two-a-day workouts at Laurier Pool
  • Guest session and mentorship with Olympian Heather MacLean — see her full profile below
  • Daily dryland activities
  • Video analysis
  • Rock climbing, escape room, Waterloo Park

Head Coaches

Ron Forrest
Assistant Head Coach, WLU

Ron began as an age-group swimmer with ROW before a 25-plus year coaching career that included two decades as Head Coach and Program Director of the Fort Worth Area Swim Team. His athletes have included an Olympic gold medallist, an Olympic qualifier and world record holder, a Para-swimming world record holder, and numerous Pan-American, Commonwealth, and NCAA competitors. He was named Texas Swimming Senior Coach of the Year in 2002 and 2004.

Tyson MacDonald
ROW Coaching Staff

A recently retired competitive swimmer who represented Canada internationally, most recently earning three medals at the 2023 Para Pan American Games in Santiago, Chile, Tyson swam at Wilfrid Laurier University from 2015 to 2020 and brings that experience into his second year coaching at ROW.

Guest Coach

Russ Franklin
Guest Coach · 51st Year Coaching

Now in his 51st year of coaching, roughly 30 of them as a head coach, Russ has guided Provincial and National medallists. His athletes have gone on to represent Canada internationally, including at the Olympics.

Special Guest

Heather MacLean
Team Canada · 2012 Olympian

Heather MacLean is a Canadian swimmer from Mississauga, Ontario. She represented Canada in the women’s 4x100m freestyle relay at the 2012 London Olympics, and earlier competed at the 2009 World Championships in Rome and the Junior Pan Pacific Championships. She swam collegiately for the University of British Columbia Thunderbirds.
