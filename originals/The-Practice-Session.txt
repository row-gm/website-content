<h2 style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:0 0 8px;font-size:24px;">The one rule</h2>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:15px;color:#152225;line-height:1.65;margin:0 0 12px;"><strong>Every session has one purpose.</strong> Name it before you write it. If you cannot say in one sentence what a session is for, the swimmers will not know either, and the set will drift towards moderate work that trains nothing well.</p>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14.5px;color:#152225;line-height:1.65;margin:0 0 12px;">A session also develops the swimmer, not only the swim. What that looks like in practice is at the bottom of this page.</p>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14.5px;color:#152225;line-height:1.65;margin:0;">The five zones and the age guidance behind them are on the How We Design Training page. This document assumes you already have the session&#39;s zone from your weekly plan.</p>
</div>

<div style="background:#FFFFFF;border:1px solid #DAD3C2;border-radius:12px;padding:24px 26px;">
<h2 style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:0 0 8px;font-size:24px;">Session template</h2>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#4B5B60;line-height:1.65;margin:0 0 14px;">A shape, not a straitjacket. Percentages are of total session distance, and assume a session of 90 minutes or more. Shorter sessions cut the pre-set and shorten the warm-up first.</p>

<div style="border:1px solid #DAD3C2;border-radius:10px;overflow:hidden;background:#FFFFFF;box-shadow:0 6px 20px rgba(10,46,63,0.10);">
<div style="display:flex;gap:16px;padding:14px 20px;background:#FFFFFF;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;align-items:baseline;">
<div style="flex:none;width:150px;font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;font-size:12.5px;letter-spacing:0.01em;color:#0A2E3F;">Warm-up</div>

<div style="flex:none;width:70px;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:12px;font-weight:700;color:#136B77;">15 to 20%</div>

<div style="flex:1;font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13.5px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;">Raise heart rate, open the shoulders and hips, and get the feel of the water. Ends faster than it starts. Mostly White and Pink.</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:16px;padding:14px 20px;background:#FAF8F2;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;align-items:baseline;">
<div style="flex:none;width:150px;font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;font-size:12.5px;letter-spacing:0.01em;color:#0A2E3F;">Skill focus</div>

<div style="flex:none;width:70px;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:12px;font-weight:700;color:#136B77;">10 to 15%</div>

<div style="flex:1;font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13.5px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;">One technical point per session, stated out loud. Drills, then the same point held while swimming. Low speed, high attention.</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:16px;padding:14px 20px;background:#FFFFFF;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;align-items:baseline;">
<div style="flex:none;width:150px;font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;font-size:12.5px;letter-spacing:0.01em;color:#0A2E3F;">Pre-set</div>

<div style="flex:none;width:70px;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:12px;font-weight:700;color:#136B77;">10%</div>

<div style="flex:1;font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13.5px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;">Bridges the warm-up and the main set. Builds towards the zone the main set will use, so the first repeat is not the throwaway one.</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:16px;padding:14px 20px;background:#FAF8F2;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;align-items:baseline;">
<div style="flex:none;width:150px;font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;font-size:12.5px;letter-spacing:0.01em;color:#0A2E3F;">Main set</div>

<div style="flex:none;width:70px;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:12px;font-weight:700;color:#136B77;">35 to 45%</div>

<div style="flex:1;font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13.5px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;">The reason the session exists. One zone, or two that support each other. This is the part you would protect if the session got cut short.</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:16px;padding:14px 20px;background:#FFFFFF;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;align-items:baseline;">
<div style="flex:none;width:150px;font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;font-size:12.5px;letter-spacing:0.01em;color:#0A2E3F;">Speed or race skills</div>

<div style="flex:none;width:70px;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:12px;font-weight:700;color:#136B77;">10%</div>

<div style="flex:1;font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13.5px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;">Starts, turns, breakouts, finishes, or short Green work. Do it before fatigue ruins the quality, or keep it deliberately short at the end.</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:16px;padding:14px 20px;background:#FAF8F2;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;align-items:baseline;">
<div style="flex:none;width:150px;font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;font-size:12.5px;letter-spacing:0.01em;color:#0A2E3F;">Cool-down</div>

<div style="flex:none;width:70px;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:12px;font-weight:700;color:#136B77;">10%</div>

<div style="flex:1;font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13.5px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;">Genuine easy swimming, not a token 100. Longer after Red and Purple sets than after Pink.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div style="border-left:4px solid #3FBFB0;background:#EFFAF8;border-radius:0 10px 10px 0;padding:16px 20px;margin:20px 0 0;font-size:14.5px;color:#152225;font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;line-height:1.6;"><strong>One main set per session.</strong> Two big main sets means neither gets done properly. If the plan needs two, one of them belongs in a different session.</div>
</div>

<div style="background:#FFFFFF;border:1px solid #DAD3C2;border-radius:12px;padding:24px 26px;">
<h2 style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:0 0 8px;font-size:24px;">How to write a set</h2>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#4B5B60;line-height:1.65;margin:0 0 14px;">One format, every deck. Reps, distance, interval, zone, focus. The interval is either a send-off (@ 1:30) or a rest interval (:15 rest). Both are written the same way.</p>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:13.5px;color:#152225;background:#FAF8F2;border:1px solid #DAD3C2;border-radius:8px;padding:12px 16px;margin:0 0 10px;line-height:1.6;"><strong>8 x 100 @ 1:30 &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; RED &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; hold 14 strokes</strong></div>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:13.5px;color:#152225;background:#FAF8F2;border:1px solid #DAD3C2;border-radius:8px;padding:12px 16px;margin:0 0 10px;line-height:1.6;">10 x 200 :15 rest &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; PINK &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; even pace, breathe every 5 on the last 50</div>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:13.5px;color:#152225;background:#FAF8F2;border:1px solid #DAD3C2;border-radius:8px;padding:12px 16px;margin:0 0 10px;line-height:1.6;">6 x 50 @ 1:30 &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; PURPLE &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; 200 goal pace, breathe every 3</div>

<div style="font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-size:13.5px;color:#152225;background:#FAF8F2;border:1px solid #DAD3C2;border-radius:8px;padding:12px 16px;margin:0 0 10px;line-height:1.6;">10 x 25 from push @ 1:00 &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; GREEN &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; max speed, 5 dolphin kicks off the wall</div>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14px;color:#152225;line-height:1.65;margin:14px 0 0;">The zone tells the swimmer how hard. The focus tells them what to think about. A set with no focus is a set the swimmer will just get through.</p>

<div style="border-left:4px solid #3FBFB0;background:#EFFAF8;border-radius:0 10px 10px 0;padding:16px 20px;margin:20px 0 0;font-size:14.5px;color:#152225;font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;line-height:1.6;">Write one interval on the board, then adjust by lane or by swimmer. Fitting the set to the individual is Layer 4, and it does not change what is written here.</div>
</div>

<p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:15px;color:#152225;line-height:1.65;margin:0 0 14px;">A send-off looks more organized. It is often less fair. <p style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:14.5px;color:#152225;line-height:1.65;margin:20px 0 12px;"><strong>Keeping it tidy.</strong> The organization concern is real. These four fixes handle almost all of it.</p>

<div style="border:1px solid #DAD3C2;border-radius:10px;overflow:hidden;background:#FFFFFF;box-shadow:0 6px 20px rgba(10,46,63,0.10);">
<div style="display:flex;gap:16px;padding:15px 20px;background:#FFFFFF;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;"><span style="flex:none;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:13px;width:28px;height:28px;border-radius:50%;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;">1</span>

<div>
<div style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;font-size:13px;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:2px 0 5px;">Regroup at round breaks</div>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13.8px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;">Rest interval inside each round, everyone back on the top of the clock between rounds. The drift never grows past one round.</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:16px;padding:15px 20px;background:#FAF8F2;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;"><span style="flex:none;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:13px;width:28px;height:28px;border-radius:50%;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;">2</span>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13.8px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;">Same set, three send-offs. Organized like a clock set, fair like a rest set. The middle ground most coaches are looking for.</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:16px;padding:15px 20px;background:#FFFFFF;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;"><span style="flex:none;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:13px;width:28px;height:28px;border-radius:50%;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;">3</span>

<div>
<div style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;font-size:13px;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:2px 0 5px;">Order the lane by speed</div>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13.8px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;">Within a lane, similar swimmers means similar cycles, so the lane stays together on its own.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex;gap:16px;padding:15px 20px;background:#FAF8F2;border-top:1px solid #DAD3C2;"><span style="flex:none;font-family:'Courier New', Courier, monospace;font-weight:700;font-size:13px;width:28px;height:28px;border-radius:50%;background:#136B77;color:#FFFFFF;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;">4</span>

<div>
<div style="font-family:'Arial Black', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;text-transform:uppercase;font-size:13px;letter-spacing:0.02em;color:#0A2E3F;margin:2px 0 5px;">Say the rest out loud each rep early on</div>

<div style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:13.8px;color:#152225;line-height:1.55;">Swimmers new to rest intervals need two or three sessions to learn to watch the clock for themselves. It is a skill, and it is worth teaching.</div>
</div>
