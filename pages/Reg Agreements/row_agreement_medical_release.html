Medical Release

I confirm that I am the parent or legal guardian of the swimmer(s) named in this registration.

I confirm that my child is physically able to take part in all ROW Swim Club activities. I must share any known medical conditions when registering each year.

Liability Waiver

By registering my child with ROW Swim Club, I agree to allow my child and family members to participate in ROW Swim Club activities, and hereby release ROW Swim Club, its directors, officers, agents, coaches, and employees from liability for any injury — including outcomes that are severe and even fatal — that may occur while my child or family members take part in ROW Swim Club activities, whether or not such damages or loss are due to negligence. This includes travel to and from training sessions, swim meets, or other team events.

I agree to indemnify and hold harmless ROW Swim Club and its associated individuals, their agents and employees, against any and all claims for personal injury or damage to property arising from participation in ROW Swim Club activities by my child or other family members.
