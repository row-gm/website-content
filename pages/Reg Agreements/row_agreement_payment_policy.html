Payment Policy – Withdrawal – FPP Agreement

This covers your fees, payment options, and the withdrawal process. It also includes your commitment to the Family Partnership Program (FPP).

Fees

Fees must be paid before your swimmer can start any ROW program. Full details of current fees are available on our Registration Fees page.

Registration Fee — Covers Swim Ontario/Swimming Canada registration and ROW administration costs. Registration fees are non-refundable.

Training Fees — Cover pool time, coaching, and lifeguards. Families with more than one swimmer receive a discount: 10% off the second swimmer's training fee, and 15% off each additional sibling's training fee.

Prepaid Meet Fees — An estimated amount for competition entry fees, charged to all competitive swimmers. Any unused portion can be carried over to the next season or refunded within 30 days — your choice.

Travel Fee — A $25 fee is added to your account for each travel meet your swimmer enters, to cover coaching costs at the meet.

Payment Options

You may pay the full amount at registration, or choose monthly payments. See the Registration Fees page for the payment schedule for your swimmer's program.

Monthly payers must set up a Pre-Authorized Debit (PAD) agreement, or send an e-transfer by the due date each month.

Credit card payments are processed securely through TeamUnify. A 3.65% processing fee is added to the total.

A $20 fee applies to all returned cheques or failed PAD transactions. It is your responsibility to keep your account current.

Withdrawal

Effective October 1st each season, to withdraw from a program, email office@rowswimming.ca. Your fees will be prorated based on the last day of the month we receive your notice, and your swimmer is welcome to continue until then.

Any remaining balance — including meet entry fees already submitted on your swimmer's behalf — is due by the end of that month. Any overpayment will be credited to your account and refunded within 30 days of the end of participation.

Outstanding Balances

Accounts with an unpaid balance at the end of the season will not be eligible to register for the following season. Transfer requests to another swimming organization will also be denied until the balance is paid in full.

Family Partnership Program (FPP)

By registering, I confirm that I have read and agree to the ROW Swim Club Family Partnership Program as posted on our website. I understand that FPP requirements are reviewed each season and that not meeting them may result in additional charges.

Agreement

I agree to pay the fees for my swimmer's program. I have read and understand the payment and withdrawal policies. I understand that swim meet and travel fees are charged in addition to registration and training fees.
