Photo and Video Consent

You give ROW permission to use photos, videos, and results of your swimmer on our website, social media, and with news media covering swimming events.

ROW Swim Club shares news and updates through our website, social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram, and X), newsletters, and with news media covering swimming events.

This consent is optional. You can complete your registration without agreeing to it.

1. I give permission for ROW Swim Club to use photographs and videos of my swimmer taken at swim meets or club events. I understand that these images, along with limited information — such as my swimmer's first name and swim group — may be shared on the ROW website, ROW social media accounts, newsletters, or with news media.

2. I give permission for ROW Swim Club to post swimming results and related information on the club website, newsletters, and social media accounts, and to share this information with news media.

3. I understand that neither my swimmer nor I will receive payment or other compensation for the use of photos, videos, or results.

4. By agreeing, I release ROW Swim Club from any liability related to the uses described above.

5. I understand that I may withdraw this consent at any time by contacting ROW Swim Club in writing. Materials already published will not be affected.
