Rowan's Law — Concussion Awareness

Under Ontario's Rowan's Law, swimmers under 26 and parents of swimmers under 18 must confirm they have reviewed the province's concussion awareness resources.

Rowan's Law is an Ontario law. It requires sport organizations to make sure athletes and their families review concussion awareness information each year before they participate.

All ROW swimmers under 26 — and a parent or guardian for any swimmer under 18 — must review and acknowledge the Rowan's Law Concussion Awareness Resources published by the Government of Ontario. This is done through a separate Swim Ontario step in the registration process. You will receive an email from Swim Ontario to complete this step. Registration is not fully active until this step is done.

You can review the resources in advance at: ontario.ca/page/rowans-law-concussion-awareness-resources

ROW Swim Club follows Swim Ontario's Concussion Management Procedure, including its removal-from-play protocol. Any swimmer suspected of having a concussion must be removed from training or competition right away and may not return until cleared through the return-to-sport protocol.
