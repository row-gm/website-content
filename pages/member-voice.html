A quick note before you continue
The people involved — whether a coach, a staff member, or another family — often share your interest in a good outcome. A direct conversation gives them a chance to respond before a formal record exists.

Submitting here is always an option, and it does not close off a direct conversation. But if you have not tried that yet, it is worth considering first.

Not sure who to contact? Email office@rowswimming.ca and we can help.
