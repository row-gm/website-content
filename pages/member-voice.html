A quick note before you continue
Most of the time, the people involved — a coach, a staff member, another family — want the same good outcome you do. A direct conversation gives everyone a chance to clear things up before a formal record is created.

You can always submit here — and that does not close the door on a conversation. But if you have not tried talking it through yet, it may be worth doing that first.

Not sure who to contact? Email office@rowswimming.ca and we can help.
