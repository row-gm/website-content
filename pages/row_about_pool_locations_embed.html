This Olympic-length 50 m pool has six lanes, two short of national and Olympic standard. It is home to ROW, the Laurier Golden Hawks swim team, the Laurier competitive lifeguarding team, prospective national team members and a number of former Olympic swimmers, alongside intramural water polo and recreational programs.

This facility does not have a nut-free policy.
