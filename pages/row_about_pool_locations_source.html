We train in some great facilities across the Kitchener-Waterloo Region.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
WLU

Wilfrid Laurier University

75 University Ave West →
Parking Out front. Observe the signs and metered parking — WLU will ticket.
Access You’ll get a card from Laurier after the season starts to access the facility.
Change Rooms Located downstairs.
Gallery On the second floor of the Athletic Complex, overlooking the pool.

This Olympic-length (50m) pool has six lanes, two short of National and Olympic standards. It’s home to ROW, the Laurier Golden Hawks swim team, the Laurier competitive lifeguarding team, prospective National Team members and many former Olympic swimmers, plus intramural water polo and recreational swim programs.

This facility does not have a nut-free policy.
