How this group fits the plan

ROW groups share one development plan. It sets out what we are developing, how training is built, and why a swimmer’s progress does not run in a straight line.

How the programs connect as swimmers develop is on Programs Overview. What we are developing, and why, is in Our Development Plan.

What to Expect

  • All four development dimensions. ROW develops Physical, Technical, Mental, and Character. At AGD 1, all four are explicit coaching goals, not background assumptions.
  • Training organized by phase. The season is built in deliberate phases. Each block has a purpose — loading, sharpening, or recovering — and the week is designed to match it.
  • Versatility and event profile. All four strokes continue to be developed. The Goal Setting Tool helps you identify where your times are strong and where the gaps are.
  • Goal setting with your coach. Swimmers use the Goal Setting Tool to set targets for the next block and the season. Those targets become part of the coaching conversation.
  • Individual development within the group. Sessions are designed for the group but coaching is adapted to each swimmer’s events, stage, and current needs.
  • The ROW Way. ROW uses swimming to graduate swimmers who are experts in themselves and their own performance. AGD 1 is where that expectation starts to be placed on the swimmer.
  • GoSwim and underwater video. Coaches use GoSwim reference clips and the underwater camera in sessions for individual stroke feedback.
