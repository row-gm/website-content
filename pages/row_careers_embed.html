Why Work at ROW?

ROW Swim Club exists to create experiences in competitive swimming that shape our swimmers, families, and coaches — long after their time with the club has ended. Every role at ROW, on deck or off, is part of that mission.

We take a holistic and professional approach to athlete development. Across all twelve training groups, coaches work with a shared language — five training zones and six training phases — so the program is consistent at every level. What we are building in every swimmer is rooted in the same twelve qualities — physical, technical, mental, and character. You can read about them on The ROW Way. Whatever your role, the work you do moves that forward.
