ROW Swim Club

Coaching at ROW

One shared approach to training across every competitive group. This hub holds the standards, the vocabulary, and the documents we all work from. Session content stays yours.

Why we built this

ROW has strong coaches. What we have not had is a shared way of describing what we do. The same training week could be explained four different ways depending on which coach you asked. That makes swimmers harder to move between groups, handovers harder to run, and family questions harder to answer well.

This framework fixes the language and the standards, not the coaching. Every group uses the same words for the same things. What happens inside those words is still built by you, for the age, stage and events of the swimmers in front of you.

What this is not. It is not one session for every group and it is not a script. You keep control of session content. What is shared is the structure, the vocabulary, and the standards a swimmer must meet to move up.

What we are asking of you

Four things. Everything else is your call as lead coach.

1
Use the six phase names and the five zones. Phases in your season plan, zone numbers on your whiteboard, both in anything you send to families. This is the one change that has to happen everywhere at once for the rest to work.
2
Name your peak meets before the season starts. Two or three per group. Share them with families in September, not in November when someone asks why their swimmer swam slow.
3
Bring your season plan to lead coach meetings. Mapped against the six phases. That is where the gaps in this framework will show up.
4
Say when something does not fit your group. A framework that only works for 18&U swimmers has failed. If a rule breaks down at 12&U, that is information we need, not a problem with your coaching.
Nothing here changes what you write on the whiteboard tomorrow. It changes what we call it, and whether the coach after you can pick it up.
