How this group fits the plan

ROW groups share one development plan. It sets out what we are developing, how training is built, and why a swimmer’s progress does not run in a straight line.

How the programs connect as swimmers develop is on Programs Overview. What we are developing, and why, is in Our Development Plan.

What to Expect

  • Technical development across all four strokes. Coaching covers the full stroke pattern and the details: head position, hand entry, kick timing.
  • Versatility as a long-term goal. All four strokes are developed in parallel. This broad base is what later specialisation is built on.
  • Competitive skills with meets in mind. Starts, turns, and basic race planning are practised as race tools.
  • Four development dimensions. ROW develops Physical, Technical, Mental, and Character — all four, in every group. JD2 is where that work begins in a structured way.
  • The ROW Way. The goal is to develop swimmers who are experts in their own performance. That does not start at the top — it starts here.
  • The step before JD1. Swimmers who are ready move into JD1 and four sessions a week.
  • GoSwim and underwater video. Coaches use GoSwim reference clips and the underwater camera in sessions for individual stroke feedback.
