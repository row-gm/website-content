Confirm or Decline

Every meet and event on your swimmer's list needs an answer. Here is what each answer does, and how to give one.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

Confirming is how your swimmer gets entered

We build every entry from the swimmers who have confirmed. So each meet and event needs one of two answers from you.

Your answer What it does
Your answerConfirm What it doesPuts your swimmer in the entry for that meet.
Your answerDecline What it doesKeeps your swimmer out of it. This is a real answer that does a real thing: it closes that meet off, so nobody is entered by accident.

Declining is not a letdown. It is the right answer when your swimmer cannot make a meet, it takes a moment, and it tells their coach what to plan for.

You can change your mind, until the deadline

Either answer can be changed as often as you like up to the Confirm By date. Confirmed and then something came up? Change it to Decline. Declined and the weekend freed up? Change it to Confirm.

On the Confirm By date the confirmation page closes. After that we submit the entry, and the club pays for every swimmer on it. That is why a change after the deadline cannot be undone or refunded.

Where to find the deadline. Every meet and event on the schedule has a Confirm By date. Your group's calendar carries the same dates.

How to answer

Three taps from the schedule. You answer in your ROW member account, not by replying to an email.

1
Tap the Confirm By date on the schedule
It takes you straight to that meet's confirmation page. Sign in if you are asked to.
2
Choose Confirm or Decline for each swimmer
Families with more than one swimmer answer for each of them separately.
3
Check it saved
Your answer shows against your swimmer's name. If it does not, it did not save.

No link on the date? That meet has not been set up for confirming yet. Sign in to your member account, open it from the team events list, and answer there.

Questions

Do we have to answer every meet on the list?
Yes, either way. Nobody is expected at every meet, and a Decline is a perfectly good answer. What we cannot work with is no answer at all.
What happens if we do not answer?
We build each entry from the swimmers who have confirmed, so a meet with no answer is not entered. Please answer either way. Declining takes a moment and it is the right answer when your swimmer cannot make it.
Can we change a Decline back to a Confirm?
Yes, any time before the Confirm By date. Neither answer is locked in until then, so answer early and change it if you need to.
We missed the deadline. Can our swimmer still race?
Ask your swimmer's coach. Sometimes a late entry is possible and sometimes it is not, and they will know quickly either way.
We confirmed and now cannot go. What should we do?
Change it to Decline if the Confirm By date has not passed. After that date, tell your coach as soon as you know, so they can adjust relays and the meet sheet. The entry fee cannot be refunded.
Our swimmer needs a qualifying time for a meet. Do we still answer?
Yes. Confirm if you would like them to race and your coach will tell you whether they have the time. Meets that need one are marked Qualifiers Only on the schedule.
We have two swimmers in different groups. Do we answer twice?
Yes, once for each swimmer. Their groups may be going to different meets, so the lists will not always match.
We cannot get into our member account.
Email the club office and we will sort it out. Do not let a sign-in problem run past a Confirm By date, and tell us before the deadline rather than after.
Not sure whether a meet suits your swimmer? Ask their coach before or after a practice. For anything to do with your account, entries or payments, email the club office at office@rowswimming.ca.
