Why FPP exists

ROW is a member-run, not-for-profit club. It relies on families to volunteer to run it — coordinating events and fundraising, planning and officiating at swim meets, and steering club operations through the Board of Directors. Volunteering helps keep costs down and keeps families connected to their child's swim career.

FPP is the points-based system that distributes that workload fairly across the membership.

2

How many points are required

Your requirement is set by swimmer group. Multi-swimmer families use the highest number among their swimmers — not the sum.

Multiple swimmers, one requirement. A family with one swimmer in TOPS 1 (4 pts) and one in Senior Development (10 pts) owes 10 points total for the account — not 14. Points also prorate if a swimmer joins or leaves mid-season.

New to competitive swimming? First-year families are exempt from the FPP requirement if they attend a New Parent Orientation and complete the Introduction to Officiating (Timekeeper) and Safety Marshal e-modules during the season. Skipping both results in a season-end charge — see Section 5 – If You Fall Short for how that’s calculated.

You must still report your activities (see section 4) so we know you have met your requirements.

How points are earned

Three ways to earn FPP: working an on-deck role at a ROW-hosted meet, at a non-ROW-hosted meet, or contributing to a variety of off-deck activities. Swim meet roles for ROW-hosted meets are tracked automatically; non-ROW-hosted meet roles must be submitted to officials@rowswimming.ca; and everything else is self-reported with a confirmation process.

On-deck: meet roles

Points scale with the level of certification and responsibility the role requires, and are earned per session worked — a full-day meet with several sessions can earn several points.

Off-deck: everything else

Board and committee roles, meet-day support away from the pool deck, clinics, chaperoning, and club events all count. Most are worth a flat number of points per season or per event. A few are worth 100% FPP or a share of it, meaning that percentage of your account’s required points rather than a fixed number. A few are marked Variable and are reviewed individually by the FPP Chair and General Manager.

Program DETAILS

  • Families are encouraged to volunteer at ROW-hosted swim meets as a priority for earning points and supporting ROW.
  • Members must meet the minimum job requirements to be accepted for a volunteer position as an Official, as outlined by Swim Ontario’s Becoming an Official process.
  • Members may recruit family members (grandparents, high-school-aged siblings, etc.) to volunteer on their behalf, provided those family members meet the same minimum job requirements.
  • At sign-up, specify the family name the points should be credited to (include the email you access the ROW website). Earning points on behalf of another member is only permitted if declared in writing at sign-up — points cannot be transferred after the fact.
  • FPP points have no cash value and expire at the end of each season. Points do not carry over to the following season.
  • Looking for unique ways to support ROW and earn FPP points at the same time? Visit our How to Get Involved page to see current club needs — from equipment and merchandise to professional services and sponsor connections.
