New to the club?

Book a new swimmer assessment and we will place your swimmer in the right group from day one. After that, the Parent Guide covers your first few months: how a meet runs, what to pack, and what the season looks like.

Understanding what your swimmer is doing

Our Development Plan section explains the approach rather than the timetable: what we are developing, why training changes through the year, why times stall for months at a time, and how your own role shifts as your swimmer grows.

Start with Our Development Plan.

The Family Participation Program

Every ROW family contributes hours through the season, and the club could not host a meet without it. No experience is needed for any role and training is provided.

The FPP Guide explains the requirement, and How to Get Involved lists every role.
