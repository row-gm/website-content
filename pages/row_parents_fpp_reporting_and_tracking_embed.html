FPP Reporting and Tracking

How Family Participation Program activities get recorded, and when you will hear about your balance.

Your part: report what you have done

You are responsible for submitting your completed FPP activities. Some things are tracked for you; everything else needs the form.

Tracked automatically

1
ROW-hosted meets — Fall First Try, ROW Novice, Dean Boles and the Cunningham Classic. Tracked automatically, nothing to submit.
2
Swimming Canada officials training — also tracked automatically and assigned to you.
3
A non-ROW meet — email officials@rowswimming.ca directly. Do not use the self-reporting form for meet roles.

You report these yourself

1
Everything that is not a meet role — committee work, chaperoning, merchandise help, the Off the Blocks meeting, the AGM, club socials and similar. Use the form below.
2
Include your family name and the email you use for the ROW website, so the points reach the right account.
3
Submit soon after the activity. Do not save them up for the end of the season; late submissions may not be accepted.

Open the FPP self-reporting form →

Meet questions go to officials@rowswimming.ca. Everything else to gm@rowswimming.ca.

Our part: track it and report back

ROW tracks FPP points centrally, so you do not need to keep your own tally. Balance updates go to every member at least three times a season.

If you fall short

Families who do not meet their requirement by the end of the season are charged for every point short, applied to the account after reporting closes. The current rate is $50 a point.

For example. A family with a 12-point requirement finishes with 8 points, so 4 short. Four times $50 is $200 charged to the account.

The policy and the rate are reviewed by the Board of Directors each season. The FPP Guide has the full requirement and how points are earned.
