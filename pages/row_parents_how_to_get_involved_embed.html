How to Get Involved

ROW is built by the people who show up. A great swim club is not built from the sidelines.

Where to start

There are many ways to contribute, from timing a lane at one of our meets to leading a social for your swimmer’s group, to connecting the club with merchandise or sponsored services. Most of them also earn Family Participation Program points.
