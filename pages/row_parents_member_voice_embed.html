Good to Know

 
No consequences — There are no consequences for raising a concern in good faith — even if it turns out to be a misunderstanding.
 
Your family account — This form is for concerns that relate to a swimmer registered to your family account.
 
Not sure where to start? — Your Group Parent Representative is a good first point of contact, especially if your concern is shared by other families in your group. They can raise collective concerns on behalf of the group so everyone does not need to submit separately.
 
You'll hear back — All submissions are reviewed by club leadership. Expect an acknowledgement within two business days.
 
Safe Sport concerns — You may contact Swim Ontario Safe Sport directly at any time — you do not need to go through ROW first.
If there is an immediate risk to a child or anyone else, contact police or child protective services first. Then submit this form or contact Swim Ontario directly.
