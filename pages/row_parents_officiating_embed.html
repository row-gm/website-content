Officiating

An essential role at every swim meet. No experience needed, and every hour counts towards your family’s FPP points.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

Why officiating matters

Swim meets are the milestones where swimmers test their training, build resilience and gain confidence. They are run almost entirely by volunteers, most of them ROW parents.

Volunteering does not just make the meet possible. It is the fastest way to learn how the sport works and how to support your own swimmer. We welcome volunteers at every level of experience and provide all the training.

Where to start

No prior experience is needed for any of these four.

Timekeeper
Operate a stopwatch and assist with the electronic timing system for one lane.

Your role is what makes an accurate swim time possible.

Safety Marshal
Keep swimmers safe during warm-up, following the Swimming Canada warm-up safety procedures. Reports to the Referee.

Sets the tone for the session and keeps everyone safe.

Marshal
Help the admin desk keep swimmers organized on deck.

Getting swimmers to their heats stops anyone missing a race, and an orderly walk to the blocks lets them focus on the swim.

Inspector of Turns
Watch swimmers at the start and through their turns to check the rules for their stroke.

You are what makes the race fair and consistent for everyone in it.

More detail on any position is on the Swim Ontario positions page.

Senior positions

Senior officials mentor and supervise other volunteers. All of them need deck experience first, and some, like Referee, need certification in several other roles.

Interested in a senior position? Get familiar with the certification pathway and email officials@rowswimming.ca so we can support you.

What is expected

Membership of any swim club in Canada comes with the expectation that families help run meets. At ROW that means three things.

1
Register as an official.
2
Attend the officiating clinics for the roles you will work.
3
Officiate at ROW meets when your swimmer is in the meet.

To work on deck you must also:

1
Be 14 or older.
2
Register each swim season, September 1 to August 31, to be an active official.
3
Complete the training for the positions you will work at a competition.
Time spent helping run meets earns Family Participation Program points. Our officials administrators will walk any new volunteer through the process — email officials@rowswimming.ca to start.

How to get started

1
Register. Complete the form shared with the ROW membership each season. Re-registration is required every year; if you did not get the link, email officials@rowswimming.ca.
2
Training. First time out, complete one or two short online courses from Swim Ontario. We send you everything you need.
3
Tell us you are available. Watch your inbox about four weeks before each meet. Registered officials are contacted first.
4
On the day. Wear a red or white shirt and black pants, shorts or a skirt. Your team gives you your assignment and any equipment.
5
Enjoy it. You are now part of the group that makes every meet possible.
