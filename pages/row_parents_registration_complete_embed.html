What Happens Next

Returning members
Your registration will be reviewed and approved by the ROW office team. You'll receive a confirmation email once your spot is confirmed.
New members
Once your registration is approved, you'll receive a separate email with sign-in instructions for your private ROW account.
Your ROW account
Your account gives you access to your family billing history, swim meet and event sign-ups, your swimmer's results and personal bests, and more. Sign in any time using the link at the top right of this page.
Training fees & pre-paid meet fees
 
Training fees will be posted to your account on October 1.
 
Payment for the combined training fees and pre-paid meet fees is due starting October 15 — either in full, or by monthly instalments.
 
As your payments come in, they will pay down your oldest charges first, starting with training fees. Once training fees are paid in full, they will be applied to swim meet charges. Your balance for training fees and swim meet fees should balance out by the end of the year (depending on how active your swimmer is with swim meets).
 
As charges for individual swim meets are applied through the season, any credit balance in your account is drawn down.
