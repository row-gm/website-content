ROW Swim Club

Welcome to Registration

Sign up is straightforward for both new and returning members. Review the steps below and you'll be done in minutes.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

What's New This Season

A couple of updates before you get started:

 
Every swimmer in every group will receive a new ROW Team T-Shirt and two new black and blue swim caps. You'll confirm your swimmer's t-shirt size during registration.
 
2026–27 Fees: Review all registration, training, and swim meet fees before you begin — Registration Fees page.
 

How to Register

1

Select your group

Choose the group confirmed in August. To request a change, email gm@rowswimming.ca.
2

Complete registration

Fill in the requested information, read and accept the agreements, and answer all required questions.
3

Pay your registration fee

Your registration fee and any outstanding balance must be paid in full to complete sign-up. Training fees are not charged until October 15.
4

You're done!

ROW office staff will review and approve your registration, assign you to the right roster and billing group, and confirm your spot.
 

What Happens Next

After you register, here's what to expect.

Your ROW account
Once registered, you'll have access to a private ROW account through this website (sign-in link, top right). Use it to track payment history, update contact info, confirm or decline swim meet attendance, and follow your swimmer's personal bests and race history.
ROW app
Download the SportsEngine Motion app for your phone — iPhone or Android.
Training fees
Annual training fees will be posted to your account on October 1 and are due on October 15. At registration, you'll choose to pay in full by October 15, or split into 9 or 10 monthly payments (due the 15th of each month).
Pre-paid swim meet fees
For all competitive groups, payment of swim meet fees are also due (full or first payment) on October 15.
Season start-up details
Information about the first week of training and team events will follow shortly after registration opens.
Club communication from coaches and staff comes by email. Set your email filters to allow ROW messages through, and consider saving them to a dedicated folder.
Questions about registration? Email Andrew at gm@rowswimming.ca.
 

On behalf of your coaches and staff — welcome, and we look forward to a great season of ROW swimming!
