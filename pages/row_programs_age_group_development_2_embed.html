About this group

The first step in our Regional pathway, for swimmers who have graduated from our Junior programs, or have been assessed and are entering with previous swimming experience. Refining technique and training skills to compete at the local and regional level. Four sessions a week across three pools.

How this group fits the plan

ROW groups share one development plan. It sets out what we are developing, how training is built, and why a swimmer’s progress does not run in a straight line.

How the programs connect as swimmers develop is on Programs Overview. What we are developing, and why, is in Our Development Plan.

What to Expect

  • Technique connected to performance. Stroke refinement continues, now with faster times as the direct measure. Underwaters, turns, and stroke mechanics are all tracked against the clock.
  • Versatility and your event profile. The Goal Setting Tool shows you how your times across strokes and distances compare. Knowing where you are strong — and where you are not — is part of development at this level.
  • Higher training standards. Punctuality, focus, and response to coaching count here in a way they do not in the Junior groups.
  • Mental and character development. Meet routines, handling results, and taking feedback are all part of what is coached.
  • Training organized by phase. The season is not random. It is built in six phases — Introduction, Accumulation, Consolidation, Intensification, Peaking and Transition — with purpose behind each one.
  • The step before AGD 1. Swimmers who are ready move into AGD 1 and five sessions a week.
  • GoSwim and underwater video. Coaches use GoSwim reference clips and the underwater camera in sessions for individual stroke feedback.
