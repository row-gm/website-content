How this group fits the plan

ROW groups share one development plan. It sets out what we are developing, how training is built, and why a swimmer’s progress does not run in a straight line.

How the programs connect as swimmers develop is on Programs Overview. What we are developing, and why, is in Our Development Plan.

What to Expect

  • Stroke mechanics, practised until they hold. Technical precision is the main focus. Sessions use deliberate repetition to build correct patterns.
  • All four strokes and versatility. All four strokes continue to be developed. The Goal Setting Tool — available to all ROW swimmers — can show you where each stroke stands relative to the others.
  • Racing skills. Starts, turns, and underwaters are practised as race tools. Swimmers begin to connect better technique to faster splits.
  • Race planning and meet preparation. How to pace a race, manage a warm-up, and race across a full meet day.
  • Mental and character development. Focus, resilience, and honest effort are coached alongside swimming skills — not treated as extras.
  • The step before the Regional pathway. JD1 leads into AGD 2.
  • GoSwim and underwater video. Coaches use GoSwim reference clips and the underwater camera in sessions for individual stroke feedback.
