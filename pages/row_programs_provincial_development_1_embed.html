How this group fits the plan

ROW groups share one development plan. It sets out what we are developing, how training is built, and why a swimmer’s progress does not run in a straight line.

How the programs connect as swimmers develop is on Programs Overview. What we are developing, and why, is in Our Development Plan.

What to Expect

  • Expert in your own performance. ROW’s goal is to graduate swimmers who are experts in themselves and their own performance. PD1 is where that expectation is fully placed on the swimmer.
  • All four development dimensions. Physical, Technical, Mental, and Character are all explicit coaching targets. Technical resilience — holding stroke at race pace, under fatigue — is one of them.
  • Versatility and event focus. All four strokes are tracked. The Goal Setting Tool and regular coach conversations help you know where to focus and what the numbers mean.
  • Training organized around championship peaking. The season is built in phases, with the provincial and Canadian Age Group calendar as the target. Peaking and race prep are structured, not improvised.
  • Goal setting. Targets are set with the tool and refined with your coach. Tracking results and adjusting across the season is part of how PD1 develops self-knowledge in the swimmer.
  • Mental skills and race strategy. Executing a race plan under pressure, managing the lead-up to a peak meet, and competing at your best when it counts are trained across the season.
  • GoSwim and underwater video. Coaches use GoSwim reference clips and the underwater camera in sessions for individual stroke feedback at race pace.
