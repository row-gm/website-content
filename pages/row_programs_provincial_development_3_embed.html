How this group fits the plan

ROW groups share one development plan. It sets out what we are developing, how training is built, and why a swimmer’s progress does not run in a straight line.

How the programs connect as swimmers develop is on Programs Overview. What we are developing, and why, is in Our Development Plan.

What to Expect

  • Technical mastery to a provincial standard. All four strokes are coached to a provincial standard. Technique is the primary focus of the group.
  • Versatility and your event profile. The Goal Setting Tool shows how your times across strokes and distances compare. At PD3, knowing your strengths and your gaps is part of the work.
  • Training organized by phase. The season is structured in six deliberate phases — Introduction, Accumulation, Consolidation, Intensification, Peaking and Transition — with the provincial competition calendar as the target.
  • Mental development. Racing at the provincial level, managing pressure, and executing a race plan are developed alongside physical and technical training.
  • Goal setting with your coach. Swimmers use the Goal Setting Tool to set next-block and season targets. Those are reviewed with your coach across the season.
  • The step before PD2. PD3 leads directly into PD2.
  • GoSwim and underwater video. Coaches use GoSwim reference clips and the underwater camera in sessions for individual stroke feedback.
