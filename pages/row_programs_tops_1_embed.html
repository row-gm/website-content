About this group

For swimmers 10 and under. Three sessions a week at Cameron Heights or WLU, with two location options to suit your schedule. Swimmers are invited to ROW-hosted introductory competitions in December and May, plus up to two additional events in nearby communities.

How this group fits the plan

ROW groups share one development plan. It sets out what we are developing, how training is built, and why a swimmer’s progress does not run in a straight line.

How the programs connect as swimmers develop is on Programs Overview. What we are developing, and why, is in Our Development Plan.

What to Expect

  • All four strokes, more consistent. Technique work continues across all four strokes. The focus shifts from learning the pattern to repeating it reliably.
  • Versatility continues. All four strokes are kept moving forward. Specialization comes later — breadth comes first.
  • Competitive skills reinforced. Starts and turns are part of every session.
  • Competition as a learning tool. Swimmers go to meets and start to understand what racing feels like.
  • Training habits. Showing up, working hard, and responding to feedback are coached here alongside stroke skills.
  • The step before JD2. Swimmers move into JD2 and three longer sessions each week.
