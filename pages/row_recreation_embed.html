How this group fits the plan

ROW groups share one development plan. It sets out what we are developing, how training is built, and why a swimmer’s progress does not run in a straight line.

How the programs connect as swimmers develop is on Programs Overview. What we are developing, and why, is in Our Development Plan.

What to Expect

  • Technique coaching every session. All four strokes are coached. Stroke instruction is part of every practice, regardless of competitive background.
  • Fitness alongside technique. Sessions include fitness-based training. The work is real — just without a competitive target attached.
  • Part of ROW. Recreation swimmers are in the same club with the same coaches as every other group. The culture on deck is the same.
  • Developing more than fitness. ROW uses swimming to develop effort, focus, and resilience. That applies here too.
  • Open to a range of swimmers. Suits those returning to swimming and those newer to structured training.
  • No meet obligations. No competition calendar, no performance expectations.
