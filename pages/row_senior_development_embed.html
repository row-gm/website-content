How this group fits the plan

ROW groups share one development plan. It sets out what we are developing, how training is built, and why a swimmer’s progress does not run in a straight line.

How the programs connect as swimmers develop is on Programs Overview. What we are developing, and why, is in Our Development Plan.

What to Expect

  • All four development dimensions. Physical, Technical, Mental, and Character are all explicit coaching goals in Senior Development. Stroke refinement and consistent effort are worked on alongside each other every session.
  • Training organized by phase. The season is built in deliberate phases. Each block has a purpose, and the weekly structure is designed around it.
  • Versatility and event profile. All four strokes continue to be developed. The Goal Setting Tool helps you and your coach identify where your times are ahead and where the work needs to go.
  • Goal setting. Swimmers use the Goal Setting Tool to set next-block targets and a season stretch goal. That feeds into regular coaching conversations.
  • A wide age range in one group. Younger and older swimmers train together. The standard on deck reflects the full range of the group.
  • A pathway choice point. Senior Development feeds into PD1 or PD2, depending on how you develop across the season.
  • GoSwim and underwater video. Coaches use GoSwim reference clips and the underwater camera in sessions for individual stroke feedback.
