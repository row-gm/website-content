About this group

Where a swimmer starts. For younger swimmers who want a first taste of competitive swimming through a club. One 40-minute session each Saturday at Cameron Heights. The program runs in three ten-week blocks across the year.

How this group fits the plan

ROW groups share one development plan. It sets out what we are developing, how training is built, and why a swimmer’s progress does not run in a straight line.

How the programs connect as swimmers develop is on Programs Overview. What we are developing, and why, is in Our Development Plan.

What to Expect

  • All four strokes. Freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, and butterfly are all introduced and coached. ROW develops versatile swimmers, and that starts here.
  • Physical fundamentals. Body position, kick, and how to move through the water. These underpin everything above.
  • Competitive skills from day one. Starts, turns, and push-offs are taught each session — the same skills used at every level above.
  • Being part of a group. Sharing a lane, following a coach, and working alongside others are part of what each session teaches.
  • Developing more than a swimmer. ROW uses swimming to develop effort, focus, and resilience. That begins here.
  • The step before TOPS 2. RSA is where the ROW competitive program starts.
