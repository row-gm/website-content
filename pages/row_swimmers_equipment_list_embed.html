What to have in your bag, group by group. Check here before you buy anything.

Before you buy

Ask your coach before or after a practice if you are unsure about anything on this list. Brands are named only where they matter.

The club gets a discount at Ly Sports, so start there.

No jammer-style suits for boys in practice. Racing suits are listed by group because the rules differ by meet level.
