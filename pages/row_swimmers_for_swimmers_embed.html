Setting a goal that is worth chasing

A goal is worth having when it is close enough to reach in one training block and specific enough to swim. The Goal Setting Tool turns one best time into a target for every other distance, and gives you the splits to get there.

Bring your numbers to your coach before or after a practice. Their target always wins — they can see your training, your stroke and your race plan, and the tool cannot.
