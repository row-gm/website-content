Board of Directors

ROW is governed by a board of parent volunteers, elected to three-year terms.

The current board

Ten parent volunteers serve on the board. Board meetings are open to every ROW member in good standing.

How the board works

1
Board members are elected to three-year terms, with a maximum of two consecutive terms.
2
Elections happen at the Annual General Meeting, usually held in January. Openings are advertised in advance.
3
Board meetings are open to every ROW member in good standing, usually on the first Wednesday of the month.
4
Want an item on the agenda? Send it to the president at least two days before the meeting.

Have a concern?

Things around the pool go wrong sometimes. This is the path we ask you to follow, and each step usually resolves it.

1
Start with your swimmer’s coach, before or after a practice, and try to resolve it directly.
2
Not satisfied with the outcome? Bring it to the Head Coach.
3
Still unresolved? Contact the Member Liaison at memberliaison@rowswimming.ca.
Operational concerns go to Andrew Moss, General Manager, at gm@rowswimming.ca. To reach all ten directors, use board@rowswimming.ca.
