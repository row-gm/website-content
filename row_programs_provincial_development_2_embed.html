How this group fits the plan

ROW groups share one development plan. It sets out what we are developing, how training is built, and why a swimmer’s progress does not run in a straight line.

How the programs connect as swimmers develop is on Programs Overview. What we are developing, and why, is in Our Development Plan.

What to Expect

  • All four development dimensions at provincial level. Physical, Technical, Mental, and Character are all explicitly coached. The standard at PD2 reflects where the pathway is heading.
  • Versatility across the season. All four strokes continue to be developed and tracked. The Goal Setting Tool shows where your times are ahead and where the work needs to go.
  • Training organized by phase. The season is built in deliberate phases. Each block has a purpose, and the week is designed to match it.
  • Goal setting and ownership. Swimmers use the Goal Setting Tool and set targets with their coach. Taking ownership of those targets is part of what PD2 develops.
  • Long-course racing. 50m pools change pacing, stroke mechanics, and race feel. Racing well in one is a specific skill that is developed here.
  • Individual development within the group. Coaching is adapted to each swimmer’s events, stage, and current needs.
  • GoSwim and underwater video. Coaches use GoSwim reference clips and the underwater camera in sessions for individual stroke feedback.
